# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes good-faith technical contributions and discussion from anyone willing to follow these rules.

## Expected behavior

- Keep discussion focused on protocol research, evidence, interoperability, and repository maintenance.
- Critique claims and evidence rather than contributors.
- Be specific when disputing a protocol interpretation and provide reproducible evidence when possible.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, copyright, licensing, and applicable law.
- Do not publish another person's private information, persistent identifiers, credentials, location history, or other sensitive material.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, or deliberately derail repository discussions.

## Research disagreements

Protocol research often produces conflicting observations. Conflicting evidence is not misconduct. Use the repository confidence system, document the disagreement, and allow claims to remain `disputed` or `unknown` until evidence resolves them.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and discussions that violate these rules, and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe.

For ordinary moderation concerns, use the relevant GitHub issue or pull request discussion without adding private information. For sensitive security/privacy reports, follow `SECURITY.md`.
