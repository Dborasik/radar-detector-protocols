# Glossary

- **BLE** — Bluetooth Low Energy.
- **GATT** — Generic Attribute Profile used by BLE services and characteristics.
- **Characteristic** — BLE value endpoint that may support read, write, notify, or indicate operations.
- **Notify** — Server-to-client BLE update without application-layer acknowledgement.
- **Observed handle** — Numeric GATT attribute handle seen on a specific device/firmware; not assumed stable.
- **Capture** — Raw or near-raw traffic recording used as protocol evidence.
- **Companion app** — Manufacturer or third-party application that communicates with the detector.
- **Protocol variant** — A hardware/firmware-specific behavior that is not safely generalized to every unit sharing a product name.
